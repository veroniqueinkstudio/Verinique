Book Your Appointment

Ready for your next tattoo? Follow our simple booking process to get started.

How to Book

01. Contact the studio through WhatsApp.

02. Share your tattoo idea, reference images, preferred placement and size.

03. Receive your personalised design quotation.

04. Review your quotation and agree on an appointment date.

05. Pay the required 50% deposit to secure your appointment.

06. Receive confirmation from the studio.

50% Appointment Deposit Policy

A 50% deposit of the agreed tattoo price is required to reserve and secure your appointment.

The remaining 50% balance is payable according to the agreed payment arrangement.

Your appointment is confirmed only after the studio has received and acknowledged your deposit.

Deposit cancellation, refund and rescheduling terms will be communicated before payment.
